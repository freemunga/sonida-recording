The SONIDA Trademark

The Concept Behind the Binary Waveform

The SONIDA Recording identity reflects the fundamental intersection of physical sound and modern computation. The two interlocking waveforms represent dynamic acoustic signals in stereo space, while the embedded binary field of 0s and 1s represents lossless digital conversion, discrete sampling, and mathematical mix clarity.
